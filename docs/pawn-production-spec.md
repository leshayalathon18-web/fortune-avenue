# Fortune Avenue Pawn Production Specification

Status: approved visual concept, pre-production engineering

Fortune Avenue uses nine colorful resin player pawns. Each sculpt is designed around a distinct silhouette, an integral display base, paint-friendly relief, and a maximum footprint suitable for the board spaces.

| Code | Pawn | Target height | Integral base | Primary production feature |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| FA-P01 | Crooked Crown | 38 mm | 30 mm round | Raised jewel settings and reinforced crown points |

## Manufacturing intent

- Material: pressure-cast or injection-cast polyurethane resin suitable for hand-painted tabletop pieces.
- Finish: satin body color, gloss gems and glass, metallic gold/silver trim, and selective translucent resin.
- Minimum detail: 1.5 mm for exposed decorative elements unless a pawn sheet specifies a thicker section.
- Edge safety: round crown tips, horns, antennae, ice crystals, and terrain details.
- Assembly target: one-piece sculpts whenever possible. If translucent inserts require separate casting, use keyed sockets hidden inside the opaque base or body.
- Base underside: flat, lightly recessed, and suitable for an optional 0.5–1 mm anti-slip pad.
- Balance: the center of gravity must remain inside the inner 70% of the base footprint.

## Prototype verification

Before production tooling, print one master of every pawn at the listed size and complete the following checks:

1. Verify that every piece stands flat without rocking.
2. Place each pawn on every board-space shape and confirm it does not obstruct adjacent spaces.
3. Perform repeated handling and a tabletop-height drop test onto wood and tile.
4. Check the Dab Valley Rig heart supports and banger joint, Area 52 beam, Fortune Key brace, sign post, penguin foot, and locomotive pipes for stress whitening or fracture.
5. Apply one complete paint master to confirm clean color separation and accessible brush angles.
6. Approve final physical tolerances only after the test masters pass.

Digital color values in `game-data/pawns.json` are visual targets, not final production paint standards. Match physical paint samples under neutral daylight before approving a factory color specification.
