# Dab Valley Rig Design References

The final Dab Valley pawn uses the user's heart-rig image as its primary silhouette reference and adapts that idea into the Fortune Avenue color system. It is an original resin-miniature interpretation rather than a reproduction of the photographed product.

## Functional references

- [MJ Arsenal Ursa Mini Dab Rig](https://mjarsenal.com/products/ursa-mini-rig) — compact incycler construction, rear-mounted path, 10 mm connection, and a dedicated quartz bucket.
- [GRAV Eclipse Rig](https://grav.com/products/grav-eclipse-rig-clear) — compact chamber, short mouthpiece, one 10 mm female joint, and one included male quartz banger.
- [GRAV Hourglass Pocket Rig](https://grav.com/products/grav-hourglass-pocket-bubbler-rig-clear) — dense compact glass, short anti-splash mouthpiece, and one 10 mm banger connection.

These examples establish the visual cues that separate the pawn from a flower bong: a compact chamber, a clearly recognizable quartz banger, and one dedicated joint rather than a bowl plus extra open ports.

## Locked Fortune Avenue topology

The final sculpt has exactly two openings:

1. One curved mouthpiece.
2. One quartz banger bucket mounted to one joint.

The top chamber cap, large ruby-fuchsia heart, small emerald internal heart, and all connecting tubes are sealed. There is no carb hole, second joint, flower bowl, open downstem, or removable accessory.

## Production adaptation

- Target height: 43 mm.
- Integral base: 30 mm round.
- The large heart is supported at two broad contacts.
- All external tubes are at least 2 mm thick at production scale.
- The banger is permanently fused to the joint for game-piece durability.
- The heart inserts use keyed sockets if cast separately in translucent resin.
